About Joe

I’m a Seattle-based product designer who moves between design, code, and team leadership.

For 20 years, I’ve helped teams make complicated services work better for people. I started with nonprofit websites, led a small design and development team, and now build tools for Veterans on VA.gov. I’m drawn to work where clearer choices can make a real difference.

How I work

Start by listening

I learn what people need, where they get stuck, and what the team can support before shaping a solution.

Make the next step clear

I use focused screens and plain language to help people move forward without extra work.

Design for different needs

I check screen-reader and enlarged-text experiences early, then fix barriers in the design and code.

Stay close to the work

I prototype in code, work alongside engineers, and use AI for routine tasks. I review the results myself.

What I work with

Figma for design; HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React for building. I check accessibility with VoiceOver, a screen reader, and axe, an automated checker. AI tools help with routine coding tasks, and I review their output.

What I’m learning

Product strategy, service design, systems thinking, and research. I’m interested in how organizations can turn what people need into services they can deliver.
