Case study 04

Cleaning up our design tools and process

Alongside feature work, I took on the upkeep that keeps a design team moving: our design history, our design tokens, and AI tools for the team.

Situation
Alongside feature work
My role
Planned and ran it all
Partners
VA design system team
Outcome
8 code changes merged

The problem

Our design files were moving to a government-approved Figma, and years of comments and ticket links could have been lost. Our code had also drifted from the design system, and every new feature made that worse.

What I did

Moved our design history safely

I scripted the move of about 1,200 comments from 31 files and fixed about 690 links across 445 tickets. Dry run, small pilot, then the full run. Then I taught another VA team to do it.

Got four apps back on the system

I replaced about 50 hard-coded values with tokens and swapped look-alike links for real components. Along the way I found real bugs, like two colors quietly broken in production.

Built AI tools for the team

My tools capture before-and-after screenshots, fill out our ticket and pull request templates, and annotate prototypes for handoff. Reviews start with work that’s already checked.

The result

~1,200 comments saved

Our review history and ticket links made it through the move.

4 apps back on the system

Shared tokens and components, with about 345 lines of dead CSS gone.

A script others can use

The VA design system team now has it for other teams.

That’s the work

I’d love to talk about how I’d help set up design on your team.
