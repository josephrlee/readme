04 · Better tools for design teams

Cleaning up our design tools and process

I helped keep our design work organized and our apps consistent. When we moved design files, I made sure years of comments and work links came with us. I also updated four apps to use the same styles and built tools to cut down on repetitive team tasks.

Situation
Moving design files; aligning 4 apps
My role
Moved design history; updated apps
Team
Partnered with the VA Design System team
Outcome
1,195 comments rebuilt; playbook shared

The problem

When our design files moved to a government-approved version of Figma, years of discussion and work-ticket links were at risk. Meanwhile, four apps had drifted from VA’s shared standards, making them less consistent and harder to maintain.

What I did

Moved design history safely

I tested a migration script with a small pilot before moving the rest, then taught another VA team to use it.

Aligned four apps with shared standards

I replaced one-off styling and custom links with shared settings and components. The cleanup also uncovered two colors that weren’t displaying correctly.

Cut repetitive team tasks

I built tools that use AI to capture screenshots, start review templates, and add notes to design handoffs.

The result

1,200 comments moved; 690 links fixed

Across 31 design files and 445 work tickets.

50 one-off styles replaced

Four apps now use shared settings and components, with about 345 lines of unused styling removed.

A process another team can use

I shared the script and showed another VA team how to move its design history.
