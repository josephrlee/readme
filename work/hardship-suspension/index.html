02 · Designing fair choices within complex rules

A way to pause debt collection, without hiding the risk

I designed the steps and wording for a new way to pause collection on VA debt, and built the review and confirmation pages. A pause can stop collection for a time, but the deadline to ask VA to forgive the debt keeps running. I made that clear before Veterans submit a request.

Situation
Pausing collection as a deadline runs
My role
Designed the flow; built key pages
Team
Research, product, 2 engineers
Outcome
Launched July 2026

The problem

A pause can offer breathing room when someone can’t afford payments, but the deadline to ask VA to forgive the debt keeps running. If the pause is denied, there may be little or no time left to request forgiveness. People needed to understand that trade-off before choosing.

What I did

Explained the trade-off before submission

I designed a separate warning screen, with a clear way to return and choose another option.

Made the request easier to answer

I replaced a long written response with a short list of time ranges the VA debt team could use.

Focused the first release

I wrote and aligned the form’s wording, then got approval from VA’s content team.

The result

Used alongside other options

In its first weeks, Veterans chose a pause alongside debt-forgiveness requests and repayment plans.

Trade-off shown before submission

People see the deadline risk before choosing to pause collection.

Shared with Veteran advocates

I demonstrated the flow to organizations that help Veterans navigate benefits.
